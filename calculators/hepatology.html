Hepatology calculators

21 hepatology calculators and scores. Each one shows its bands and its source, and works offline.

  • MELD

    Liver disease severity.

    Source: Kamath PS et al, Hepatology 2001.

  • FIB-4

    Fibrosis index in fatty liver.

    Source: Sterling RK et al, Hepatology 2006.

  • AST to ALT ratio

    Pattern in liver injury.

    Source: Standard hepatology derivation.

  • Glasgow-Blatchford score

    Risk after upper GI bleeding, before endoscopy.

    Source: Blatchford O et al, Lancet 2000.

  • Rockall score

    Mortality risk after upper GI bleeding.

    Source: Rockall TA et al, Gut 1996.

  • AIMS65

    Mortality in upper GI bleeding.

    Source: Saltzman JR et al, Gastrointest Endosc 2011.

  • Child-Pugh

    Cirrhosis severity and prognosis.

    Source: Pugh RNH et al, Br J Surg 1973.

  • BISAP

    Severity of acute pancreatitis at 24 hours.

    Source: Wu BU et al, Gut 2008.

  • MELD-Na

    MELD with sodium — 90-day mortality in cirrhosis and transplant priority.

    Source: Kim WR et al, N Engl J Med 2008; OPTN/UNOS policy 2016.

  • MELD 3.0

    Updated MELD adding sex and albumin; current US transplant allocation score.

    Source: Kim WR et al, Gastroenterology 2021.

  • ALBI grade

    Albumin–bilirubin liver function grade, validated in hepatocellular carcinoma.

    Source: Johnson PJ et al, J Clin Oncol 2015.

  • APRI

    AST to platelet ratio index — non-invasive fibrosis estimate.

    Source: Wai CT et al, Hepatology 2003; WHO hepatitis B guidelines 2015.

  • NAFLD fibrosis score

    Advanced fibrosis risk in fatty liver from routine data.

    Source: Angulo P et al, Hepatology 2007.

  • BARD score

    BMI, AST/ALT ratio and diabetes — screens fatty liver for advanced fibrosis.

    Source: Harrison SA et al, Gut 2008.

  • R factor (liver injury pattern)

    ALT and ALP, each relative to normal, classify liver injury as hepatocellular, mixed or cholestatic.

    Source: Bénichou C, J Hepatol 1990 (CIOMS consensus); Danan G, Bénichou C, J Clin Epidemiol 1993 (RUCAM).

  • Maddrey discriminant function

    Severity of alcohol-related hepatitis from prothrombin time and bilirubin.

    Source: Maddrey WC et al, Gastroenterology 1978; Carithers RL et al, Ann Intern Med 1989.

  • Glasgow alcoholic hepatitis score

    Five routine variables predict 28- and 84-day survival in alcohol-related hepatitis.

    Source: Forrest EH et al, Gut 2005; Forrest EH et al, Gut 2007.

  • Lille model

    Response to corticosteroids in severe alcohol-related hepatitis after 7 days.

    Source: Louvet A et al, Hepatology 2007.

  • Ranson criteria (non-gallstone pancreatitis)

    Five admission and six 48-hour criteria predict severity of acute pancreatitis.

    Source: Ranson JHC et al, Surg Gynecol Obstet 1974.

  • Harmless acute pancreatitis score (HAPS)

    Identifies, on admission, acute pancreatitis that will run a non-severe course.

    Source: Lankisch PG et al, Clin Gastroenterol Hepatol 2009.

  • Forrest classification

    Endoscopic appearance of a peptic ulcer predicts rebleeding and need for haemostasis.

    Source: Forrest JA, Finlayson ND, Shearman DJ, Lancet 1974; Laine L, Peterson WL, N Engl J Med 1994.
