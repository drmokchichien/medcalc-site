Endocrine calculators

15 endocrine calculators and scores. Each one shows its bands and its source, and works offline.

  • Average glucose from HbA1c

    Estimated mean glucose.

    Source: Nathan DM et al, Diabetes Care 2008.

  • HbA1c from average glucose

    Reverse of the ADAG equation.

    Source: Nathan DM et al, Diabetes Care 2008.

  • FINDRISC (diabetes risk)

    Finnish Diabetes Risk Score — 10-year risk of type 2 diabetes.

    Source: Lindström J, Tuomilehto J, Diabetes Care 2003.

  • ADA diabetes risk test

    American Diabetes Association screening score for undiagnosed type 2 diabetes.

    Source: Bang H et al, Ann Intern Med 2009.

  • HOMA-IR

    Homeostasis model assessment of insulin resistance from fasting glucose and insulin.

    Source: Matthews DR et al, Diabetologia 1985.

  • QUICKI

    Quantitative insulin sensitivity check index from fasting glucose and insulin.

    Source: Katz A et al, J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2000.

  • Aldosterone-renin ratio (ARR)

    Screening ratio for primary aldosteronism (aldosterone ng/dL ÷ plasma renin activity).

    Source: Funder JW et al, Endocrine Society guideline, J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2016.

  • Calculated free testosterone (Vermeulen)

    Free testosterone from total testosterone, SHBG and albumin.

    Source: Vermeulen A et al, J Clin Endocrinol Metab 1999.

  • Free androgen index (FAI)

    Total testosterone relative to SHBG — biochemical hyperandrogenism in women.

    Source: Wilke TJ, Utley DJ, Clin Chem 1987; International PCOS guideline 2023.

  • Modified Ferriman-Gallwey score

    Hirsutism severity across nine androgen-sensitive body areas.

    Source: Hatch R et al, Am J Obstet Gynecol 1981.

  • Rotterdam criteria (PCOS)

    Polycystic ovary syndrome: two of three features after excluding mimics.

    Source: Rotterdam ESHRE/ASRM consensus, Hum Reprod 2004; Teede HJ et al, International PCOS guideline 2023.

  • OST / OSTA (osteoporosis self-assessment)

    Osteoporosis Self-assessment Tool for Asians — weight and age only.

    Source: Koh LK et al, Osteoporos Int 2001.

  • ORAI (osteoporosis risk)

    Osteoporosis Risk Assessment Instrument for selecting women for bone densitometry.

    Source: Cadarette SM et al, CMAJ 2000.

  • SCORE (osteoporosis)

    Simple Calculated Osteoporosis Risk Estimation for women.

    Source: Lydick E et al, Am J Manag Care 1998.

  • Burch-Wartofsky point scale (thyroid storm)

    Likelihood of thyroid storm in a thyrotoxic patient.

    Source: Burch HB, Wartofsky L, Endocrinol Metab Clin North Am 1993.
