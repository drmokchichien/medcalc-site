Neurology calculators

23 neurology calculators and scores. Each one shows its bands and its source, and works offline.

  • Cerebral perfusion pressure

    MAP minus intracranial pressure.

    Source: Standard neurocritical care derivation.

  • Modified Rankin Scale

    Global disability after stroke (0–6).

    Source: van Swieten JC et al, Stroke 1988.

  • Hunt and Hess grade

    Clinical severity of subarachnoid haemorrhage.

    Source: Hunt WE, Hess RM, J Neurosurg 1968.

  • WFNS SAH grade

    Subarachnoid haemorrhage grade from GCS and motor deficit.

    Source: Teasdale GM et al (WFNS committee), J Neurol Neurosurg Psychiatry 1988.

  • Modified Fisher scale

    CT blood load in SAH, predicting vasospasm.

    Source: Frontera JA et al, Neurosurgery 2006.

  • ICH score

    30-day mortality after spontaneous intracerebral haemorrhage.

    Source: Hemphill JC et al, Stroke 2001.

  • ICH volume (ABC/2)

    Bedside haematoma volume from CT dimensions.

    Source: Kothari RU et al, Stroke 1996.

  • ASPECTS

    Early ischaemic change on CT in the MCA territory (10 = normal).

    Source: Barber PA et al, Lancet 2000.

  • SPAN-100 index

    Age plus NIHSS to predict outcome after thrombolysis.

    Source: Saposnik G et al, Neurology 2013.

  • DRAGON score

    3-month outcome after IV thrombolysis for ischaemic stroke.

    Source: Strbian D et al, Neurology 2012.

  • SEDAN score

    Risk of symptomatic haemorrhage after IV thrombolysis.

    Source: Strbian D et al, Ann Neurol 2012.

  • Essen stroke risk score

    Annual risk of recurrent stroke after ischaemic stroke.

    Source: Weimar C et al, Stroke 2009.

  • A²DS² score

    Risk of stroke-associated pneumonia.

    Source: Hoffmann S et al, Stroke 2012.

  • Glasgow coma scale

    Eyes, verbal and motor.

    Source: Teasdale G, Jennett B, Lancet 1974.

  • FOUR score

    Coma scale that keeps a brainstem component.

    Source: Wijdicks EFM et al, Ann Neurol 2005.

  • NIH stroke scale

    Stroke deficit score, fifteen items.

    Source: Brott T et al, Stroke 1989.

  • ABCD²

    Stroke risk after a transient ischaemic attack.

    Source: Johnston SC et al, Lancet 2007.

  • PHQ-9 depression

    Depression severity over the last two weeks, nine items scored 0 to 3.

    Source: Kroenke K et al, J Gen Intern Med 2001.

  • GAD-7 anxiety

    Anxiety severity over the last two weeks, seven items scored 0 to 3.

    Source: Spitzer RL et al, Arch Intern Med 2006.

  • CAGE

    Alcohol problem screen.

    Source: Ewing JA, JAMA 1984.

  • AUDIT-C

    Three-question alcohol screen.

    Source: Bush K et al, Arch Intern Med 1998.

  • Braden pressure ulcer

    Pressure injury risk from six subscales, each scored 1 to 4.

    Source: Bergstrom N et al, Res Nurs Health 1987.

  • Morse fall scale

    Inpatient falls risk, weighted per item.

    Source: Morse JM et al, 1989.
