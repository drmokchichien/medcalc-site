  • Ejection fraction

    Linear correction at higher rates.

  • Rate-pressure product

    Myocardial oxygen demand index.

  • ORBIT bleeding risk (AF)

  • HEART score

  • TIMI (NSTEMI)

    Heart failure severity after MI.

  • Diamond-Forrester pretest probability

    Pretest probability of coronary disease from age, sex and pain type.

  • GRACE score (in-hospital mortality)

    In-hospital death risk in acute coronary syndrome from eight admission variables.

  • Revised Geneva score (PE)

    Clinical probability of pulmonary embolism without physician gestalt.

    Source: Le Gal G et al, Ann Intern Med 2006.

  • PESI (Pulmonary Embolism Severity Index)

    30-day mortality class in confirmed pulmonary embolism.

    Source: Aujesky D et al, Am J Respir Crit Care Med 2005.

  • Bova score (normotensive PE)

    Risk of PE-related complications in haemodynamically stable pulmonary embolism.

    Source: Bova C et al, Eur Respir J 2014.

  • Hestia criteria (PE outpatient treatment)

    Any positive criterion excludes outpatient treatment of pulmonary embolism.

    Source: Zondag W et al, J Thromb Haemost 2011.

  • YEARS algorithm (PE)

    Three clinical items set the D-dimer cut-off that excludes pulmonary embolism.

    Source: van der Hulle T et al, Lancet 2017.

  • Age-adjusted D-dimer cut-off

    D-dimer threshold for excluding PE: age × 10 over 50, otherwise 500.

    Source: Righini M et al, JAMA 2014 (ADJUST-PE).

  • ADD-RS (aortic dissection detection risk score)

    Pretest risk of acute aortic syndrome from three high-risk feature groups.

    Source: Rogers AM et al, Circulation 2011; Nazerian P et al, Circulation 2018 (ADvISED).

  • NYHA functional class

    Heart failure symptom severity by limitation of physical activity.

    Source: Criteria Committee of the New York Heart Association, 9th ed 1994.

  • Duke treadmill score

    Prognosis from Bruce exercise time, ST deviation and exercise angina.

    Source: Mark DB et al, Ann Intern Med 1987; Mark DB et al, N Engl J Med 1991.

  • ASCVD 10-year risk (Pooled Cohort Equations)

    10-year risk of first heart attack or stroke, ages 40 to 79.

    Source: Goff DC Jr et al, 2013 ACC/AHA Guideline on the Assessment of Cardiovascular Risk, Circulation 2014.

  • Framingham general CVD 10-year risk

    10-year risk of any cardiovascular event, ages 30 to 74 (lipid model).

    Source: D’Agostino RB Sr et al, Circulation 2008.

  • Sokolow-Lyon index (LVH)

    ECG voltage criterion for left ventricular hypertrophy: S in V1 plus the taller R in V5 or V6.

    Source: Sokolow M, Lyon TP, Am Heart J 1949.

  • Cornell voltage-duration product (LVH)

    ECG LVH criterion: (R aVL + S V3, plus 6 mm in women) × QRS duration.

  • QTc (Hodges)

    Linear heart-rate correction of the QT interval: QT + 1.75 × (HR − 60).

    Source: Hodges M et al, J Am Coll Cardiol 1983.

  • San Francisco Syncope Rule

    CHESS criteria identify syncope patients at risk of serious 7-day outcomes.

    Source: Quinn JV et al, Ann Emerg Med 2004.

  • Canadian Syncope Risk Score

    30-day serious adverse event risk after ED syncope.

    Source: Thiruganasambandamoorthy V et al, CMAJ 2016.

  • H₂FPEF score

    Probability of heart failure with preserved ejection fraction in unexplained breathlessness.

    Source: Reddy YNV et al, Circulation 2018.

  • EDACS (Emergency Department Assessment of Chest pain Score)

    Identifies chest pain patients safe for early discharge with 0 and 2 hour troponins.

    Source: Than M et al, Emerg Med Australas 2014.

  • CRUSADE bleeding score

    In-hospital major bleeding risk in NSTEMI.

    Source: Subherwal S et al, Circulation 2009.

  • Wilkins score (mitral valve)

    Echo morphology score predicting success of percutaneous balloon mitral valvotomy.

    Source: Wilkins GT et al, Br Heart J 1988.

  • CCS angina class

    Canadian Cardiovascular Society grading of angina severity.

    Source: Campeau L, Circulation 1976.

  • Ankle-brachial index

    Highest ankle systolic pressure in one leg divided by the higher arm pressure.

    Source: Gerhard-Herman MD et al, 2016 AHA/ACC Guideline on Lower Extremity PAD, Circulation 2017.

  • DAPT score

    Benefit versus bleeding of continuing dual antiplatelet therapy beyond 12 months after PCI.

    Source: Yeh RW et al, JAMA 2016.
