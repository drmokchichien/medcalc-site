Common questions

Does it work without signal? Yes. All 151 calculators run on the device, no connection needed.

Do I need an account? No. There is no login and no subscription; nothing is paywalled.

Where do the formulas come from? Each calculator shows its source under the inputs, and the "About" tab lists the references. Check them against your local guidelines.

Something looks wrong. Email us with a screenshot. We correct errors and credit the reporter in the release notes.

Can I remove ads? Not currently — ads keep it free. The sponsor line on the home screen is sold in blocks.

Disclaimer

MedCalc is a decision aid for clinicians and students. It does not replace clinical judgement, and it is not a diagnosis. Confirm doses and cut-offs locally, especially in renal impairment, pregnancy and paediatrics.
