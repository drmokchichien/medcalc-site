Obstetrics calculators

21 obstetrics calculators and scores. Each one shows its bands and its source, and works offline.

  • Estimated delivery date

    Naegele from the last menstrual period.

    Source: Naegele rule.

  • Gestational age

    Weeks and days from the last period.

    Source: Standard obstetric dating.

  • Estimated fetal weight

    Hadlock from head and abdominal circumference and femur length.

    Source: Hadlock FP et al, Am J Obstet Gynecol 1985.

  • Modified Bishop score (Calder)

    Cervical favourability using cervical length instead of effacement.

    Source: Calder AA, Embrey MP, Tait T, Br J Obstet Gynaecol 1977; NICE NG207 Inducing labour, 2021.

  • Robson ten-group classification

    Assigns every birth to one of ten mutually exclusive groups for caesarean audit.

    Source: Robson MS, Fetal Matern Med Rev 2001; WHO Statement on Caesarean Section Rates, 2015.

  • Gestational age from crown–rump length

    First-trimester dating from fetal crown–rump length.

    Source: Robinson HP, Fleming JE, Br J Obstet Gynaecol 1975; NICE NG201 Antenatal care, 2021.

  • Gestational age from head circumference

    Second-trimester dating when CRL exceeds 84 mm.

    Source: Hadlock FP et al, AJR Am J Roentgenol 1982; NICE NG201 Antenatal care, 2021.

  • Amniotic fluid index

    Sum of the deepest vertical pocket in each of four uterine quadrants.

    Source: Phelan JP et al, J Reprod Med 1987.

  • PAPP-A multiple of the median

    Measured PAPP-A divided by the median for gestation.

    Source: RCOG Green-top Guideline 31, The Investigation and Management of the Small-for-Gestational-Age Fetus, 2014.

  • sFlt-1/PlGF ratio

    Angiogenic ratio to rule out or rule in pre-eclampsia in suspected cases.

    Source: Zeisler H et al (PROGNOSIS), N Engl J Med 2016; Verlohren S et al, Hypertension 2014; NICE DG49, 2022.

  • Quantitative blood loss

    Measured blood loss from weighed swabs plus suction, minus non-blood fluid.

    Source: ACOG Committee Opinion 794, Quantitative Blood Loss in Obstetric Hemorrhage, 2019; RCOG Green-top Guideline 52, 2016.

  • Recommended gestational weight gain (IOM)

    Pre-pregnancy BMI and the matching total weight-gain target for a singleton pregnancy.

    Source: Institute of Medicine, Weight Gain During Pregnancy: Reexamining the Guidelines, 2009.

  • fullPIERS

    Risk of adverse maternal outcome within 48 hours in women admitted with pre-eclampsia.

    Source: von Dadelszen P et al, Lancet 2011;377:219–27.

  • Oxytocin infusion rate

    Pump rate in mL/h for a target oxytocin dose in milliunits per minute.

    Source: Standard infusion arithmetic; NICE NG235 Intrapartum care, 2023.

  • Magnesium sulfate infusion rate

    Pump rate for a magnesium sulfate maintenance dose in severe pre-eclampsia or eclampsia.

    Source: Eclampsia Trial Collaborative Group, Lancet 1995; NICE NG133 Hypertension in pregnancy, 2019.

  • Cerebroplacental ratio

    Middle cerebral artery PI divided by umbilical artery PI; a low ratio signals fetal redistribution.

    Source: Gramellini D et al, Obstet Gynecol 1992;79:416–20.

  • MCA peak systolic velocity (MoM)

    Fetal middle cerebral artery PSV as a multiple of the median, to detect fetal anaemia.

    Source: Mari G et al, N Engl J Med 2000;342:9–14.

  • Urine protein:creatinine ratio in pregnancy

    Spot protein:creatinine ratio to quantify proteinuria in hypertensive pregnancy.

    Source: NICE NG133 Hypertension in pregnancy, 2019.

  • Edinburgh postnatal depression (EPDS)

    Postnatal depression over the last seven days, ten items scored 0 to 3.

    Source: Cox JL et al, Br J Psychiatry 1987.

  • Apgar score

    Newborn condition at one and five minutes.

    Source: Apgar V, Curr Res Anesth Analg 1953.

  • Bishop score

    Cervical readiness for induction.

    Source: Bishop EH, Obstet Gynecol 1964.
