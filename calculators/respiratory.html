Respiratory calculators

29 respiratory calculators and scores. Each one shows its bands and its source, and works offline.

  • A–a gradient

    Alveolar–arterial oxygen difference.

    Source: Standard gas exchange derivation.

  • P/F ratio

    Oxygenation for ARDS grading.

    Source: ARDS Definition Task Force, JAMA 2012.

  • S/F ratio

    SpO₂-based oxygen saturation ratio.

    Source: Rice TW et al, Chest 2007.

  • ROX index

    Failure risk on high-flow nasal oxygen.

    Source: Roca O et al, Am J Respir Crit Care Med 2016.

  • Lung-protective tidal volume

    6 mL/kg of predicted body weight.

    Source: ARDSNet, N Engl J Med 2000.

  • Arterial oxygen content

    Carried and dissolved oxygen.

    Source: Standard gas derivation.

  • Oxygen delivery

    Cardiac output times arterial content.

    Source: Standard oxygen transport derivation.

  • mMRC dyspnoea scale

    Grades breathlessness in daily activity (0–4).

    Source: Bestall JC et al, Thorax 1999; GOLD report 2024.

  • FEV₁/FVC ratio

    Post-bronchodilator ratio that defines airflow obstruction.

    Source: GOLD report 2024; Stanojevic S et al, ATS/ERS interpretation, Eur Respir J 2022.

  • FEV₁ % predicted (GOLD grade)

    Grades airflow limitation severity once obstruction is confirmed.

    Source: GOLD report 2024.

  • Peak flow % of best or predicted

    Acute asthma severity from peak expiratory flow.

    Source: BTS/SIGN British guideline on the management of asthma 2019.

  • Oxygenation index

    Ventilated oxygenation severity accounting for mean airway pressure.

    Source: Pediatric Acute Lung Injury Consensus Conference (PALICC), Pediatr Crit Care Med 2015.

  • Oxygen saturation index

    Non-invasive surrogate of oxygenation index using SpO₂.

    Source: Pediatric Acute Lung Injury Consensus Conference (PALICC), Pediatr Crit Care Med 2015.

  • Minute ventilation

    Tidal volume times respiratory rate.

    Source: West JB, Respiratory Physiology: The Essentials, 10th ed.

  • Dead-space fraction (Bohr–Enghoff)

    Fraction of each breath not taking part in gas exchange.

    Source: Enghoff H, Upsala Läk Förh 1938; Nuckton TJ et al, N Engl J Med 2002.

  • DECAF score

    In-hospital mortality in acute exacerbation of COPD.

    Source: Steer J, Gibson J, Bourke SC, Thorax 2012.

  • Berlin ARDS criteria

    Whether all four Berlin criteria for ARDS are met.

    Source: ARDS Definition Task Force, JAMA 2012.

  • Light’s criteria

    Classifies a pleural effusion as exudate or transudate.

    Source: Light RW et al, Ann Intern Med 1972.

  • STOP-Bang

    Screens for obstructive sleep apnoea.

    Source: Chung F et al, Anesthesiology 2008.

  • Epworth sleepiness scale

    Self-rated daytime sleepiness across eight situations.

    Source: Johns MW, Sleep 1991.

  • Wells score (PE)

    Pre-test probability of pulmonary embolism, weighted per item.

    Source: Wells PS et al, Thromb Haemost 2000.

  • PERC rule

    Excludes PE without testing in low-risk patients.

    Source: Kline JA et al, J Thromb Haemost 2004.

  • sPESI

    30-day mortality risk in confirmed PE.

    Source: Jiménez D et al, Arch Intern Med 2010.

  • CURB-65

    Pneumonia severity and admission threshold.

    Source: Lim WS et al, Thorax 2003.

  • CRB-65

    Pneumonia severity without a blood test.

    Source: Bauer TT et al, Eur Respir J 2006.

  • BODE index

    COPD prognosis from BMI, obstruction, dyspnoea and exercise.

    Source: Celli BR et al, N Engl J Med 2004.

  • CAT (COPD)

    COPD assessment test: eight items scored 0 to 5 over a total of 40.

    Source: Jones PW et al, Eur Respir J 2009.

  • Centor / McIsaac

    Streptococcal pharyngitis probability.

    Source: McIsaac WJ et al, CMAJ 1998.

  • FeverPAIN

    Bacterial sore throat likelihood.

    Source: Little P et al, BMJ 2013.
