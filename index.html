How it works

Nothing here is a black box. Each calculator shows the formula or the score's published bands, the units, and the citation. Bands are colour-coded, and the app says explicitly where a score is not valid — for example where chronicity or age rules the result out.

Built for the ward

Offline by default, no account and no tracking. Adult and paediatric sets are separated so a tap in a hurry does not give the wrong one. Free, supported by a sponsor line and banner slots; there is no subscription.

MedCalc is a reference tool, not a medical record. Confirm against local guidelines and your own clinical judgement.
