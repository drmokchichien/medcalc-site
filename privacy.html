MedCalc Privacy Policy

Mediwira Group · Last updated 20 October 2026

The short version: MedCalc works offline. No account. Your calculations stay on your phone.

Who we are

MedCalc is published by Mediwira Group, Johor Bahru, Malaysia ("Mediwira", "we"). Contact: hello@mediwira.com.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your preference for light or dark mode, your disclaimer acknowledgement, and any calculators you pin as favourites.
  • These are held locally in the app's own storage. We cannot read them.

What we collect

Nothing personally identifying is required to use MedCalc. There is no sign-up, no login, no cloud sync and no analytics that identifies you.

Advertising

MedCalc is free and is supported by advertising, including a sponsor line on the home screen. Banner slots may be served by a third-party advertising network. Where such a network is active, it may receive:

  • A device advertising identifier and coarse device information.
  • Which ad was shown, and whether it was tapped.

Health values you type into calculators are not sent to advertisers. When an advertising partner is added, this policy lists the partner before it goes live.

Health data

MedCalc is a clinical reference and calculator tool. Values you enter stay in the app. The app does not transmit them to us, so nothing here is a medical record. Always confirm against local guidelines and your own clinical judgement.

Children

MedCalc is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored.
  • Limit ad tracking through iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and material changes are noted in the app's release notes.
