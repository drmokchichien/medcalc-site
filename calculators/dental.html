Dental calculators

32 dental calculators and scores. Each one shows its bands and its source, and works offline.

  • PUFA index

    Counts teeth with untreated caries that has reached the pulp or caused infection.

    Source: Monse B et al, Community Dent Oral Epidemiol 2010.

  • ICDAS II caries code

    Visual severity code for a single coronal caries lesion.

    Source: Ismail AI et al, Community Dent Oral Epidemiol 2007 (ICDAS II).

  • Basic periodontal examination

    Highest BPE code across the six sextants guides periodontal workup.

    Source: British Society of Periodontology, The Good Practitioner’s Guide to Periodontology 2016 / BPE guidance 2019.

  • Simplified oral hygiene index (OHI-S)

    Debris plus calculus scores on six index surfaces.

    Source: Greene JC, Vermillion JR, J Am Dent Assoc 1964.

  • Plaque control record (O’Leary)

    Percentage of tooth surfaces with disclosed plaque at the gingival margin.

    Source: O’Leary TJ, Drake RB, Naylor JE, J Periodontol 1972.

  • Care index

    Share of caries experience that has been restored (F ÷ DMFT).

    Source: Walsh J, N Z Dent J 1970; WHO Oral Health Surveys.

  • ANB angle (Steiner)

    Sagittal jaw relationship from SNA and SNB on a lateral cephalogram.

    Source: Steiner CC, Am J Orthod 1953.

  • Tweed triangle (FMIA)

    Frankfort–mandibular incisor angle from FMA and IMPA (the three sum to 180°).

    Source: Tweed CH, Angle Orthod 1954.

  • Periodontitis stage (2017 classification)

    Stage from worst interdental attachment loss and teeth lost to periodontitis.

    Source: Tonetti MS, Greenwell H, Kornman KS, J Periodontol 2018 (AAP/EFP World Workshop).

  • Periodontitis grade (bone loss ÷ age)

    Rate of progression from percentage bone loss at the worst tooth divided by age.

    Source: Papapanou PN et al, J Periodontol 2018 (AAP/EFP World Workshop).

  • Basic erosive wear examination (BEWE)

    Sum of the worst erosive wear score in each sextant.

    Source: Bartlett D, Ganss C, Lussi A, Clin Oral Investig 2008.

  • Dental aesthetic index (DAI)

    Weighted sum of ten occlusal traits that grades orthodontic treatment need.

    Source: Cons NC, Jenny J, Kohout FJ, DAI: The Dental Aesthetic Index, University of Iowa 1986; WHO Oral Health Surveys 1997.

  • DMFT index

    Permanent dentition caries experience.

    Source: WHO Oral Health Surveys, 5th edition.

  • dmft index

    Primary dentition caries experience.

    Source: WHO Oral Health Surveys, 5th edition.

  • Plaque index (Silness-Löe)

    Plaque score across four sites of a tooth.

    Source: Silness J, Löe H, Acta Odontol Scand 1964.

  • Gingival index (Löe-Silness)

    Gingival inflammation across four sites.

    Source: Löe H, Silness J, Acta Odontol Scand 1963.

  • Bleeding on probing

    Percentage of sites that bleed.

    Source: Ainamo J, Bay I, Int Dent J 1975.

  • Clinical attachment loss

    Probing depth plus recession.

    Source: 2018 periodontal classification.

  • Little’s irregularity index

    Sum of five contact displacements.

    Source: Little RM, Am J Orthod 1975.

  • Bolton anterior ratio

    Lower to upper anterior tooth width.

    Source: Bolton WA, Angle Orthod 1958.

  • Bolton overall ratio

    Lower to upper twelve tooth width.

    Source: Bolton WA, Angle Orthod 1958.

  • Pont’s index (premolar)

    Required arch width from incisor sum.

    Source: Pont A, 1909.

  • Overjet

    Horizontal incisor overlap.

    Source: British Standards Institution incisor classification.

  • Overbite

    Vertical incisor overlap.

    Source: British Standards Institution incisor classification.

  • Carey’s arch length analysis

    Available minus required arch length.

    Source: Carey CW, Am J Orthod 1958.

  • Root caries index

    Root surfaces affected per surface at risk.

    Source: Katz RV, J Public Health Dent 1980.

  • Dental anxiety (MDAS)

    Modified dental anxiety scale, five items each scored 1 to 5.

    Source: Humphris GM et al, Br Dent J 1995.

  • Tooth mobility (Miller)

    Mobility grade.

    Source: Miller SC, 1950.

  • Furcation (Glickman)

    Furcation involvement grade.

    Source: Glickman I, 1953.

  • IOTN dental health component

    Orthodontic treatment need, grades 1 to 5.

    Source: Brook PH, Shaw WC, Eur J Orthod 1989.

  • IOTN aesthetic component

    Aesthetic need from the ten-photo scale.

    Source: Brook PH, Shaw WC, Eur J Orthod 1989.

  • Maximum local anaesthetic dose

    Weight-based ceiling and 2% cartridge count.

    Source: Malaysian dental prescribing practice; manufacturer limits.
