Renal calculators

17 renal calculators and scores. Each one shows its bands and its source, and works offline.

  • eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021)

    Race-free eGFR with CKD stage.

    Source: Inker LA et al, N Engl J Med 2021.

  • eGFR (MDRD)

    Older eGFR equation, still in some labs.

    Source: Levey AS et al, Ann Intern Med 1999.

  • Creatinine clearance

    Cockcroft-Gault, the figure drug labels use.

    Source: Cockcroft DW, Gault MH, Nephron 1976.

  • Fractional excretion of sodium

    Distinguishes pre-renal from intrinsic injury.

    Source: Espinel CH, JAMA 1976.

  • Fractional excretion of urea

    Like FENa, but holds up on diuretics.

    Source: Kaplan AA, Carvounis CP, Kidney Int 1991.

  • Free water deficit

    Water needed to correct hypernatraemia.

    Source: Standard hypernatraemia derivation.

  • Sodium deficit

    Sodium needed in hyponatraemia.

    Source: Standard dysnatraemia derivation.

  • Urine output per kg per hour

    Urine output normalised to weight and time, as used in KDIGO AKI staging.

    Source: KDIGO Clinical Practice Guideline for Acute Kidney Injury, 2012.

  • KDIGO AKI stage

    Acute kidney injury stage from creatinine rise, urine output and need for dialysis.

    Source: KDIGO Clinical Practice Guideline for Acute Kidney Injury, 2012.

  • KDIGO CKD risk (GFR and albuminuria)

    Heat-map risk category combining eGFR and urine albumin:creatinine ratio.

    Source: KDIGO Clinical Practice Guideline for the Evaluation and Management of CKD, 2012 and 2024.

  • BUN to creatinine ratio

    Blood urea nitrogen to creatinine ratio from SI units, to separate prerenal from intrinsic causes.

  • eGFR (CKD-EPI cystatin C 2012)

    GFR estimate from serum cystatin C, independent of muscle mass.

    Source: Inker LA et al, N Engl J Med 2012;367:20–9.

  • eGFR (CKD-EPI creatinine–cystatin C 2021)

    Race-free combined creatinine and cystatin C GFR estimate.

    Source: Inker LA et al, N Engl J Med 2021;385:1737–49.

  • Total body water (Watson)

    Anthropometric estimate of total body water, used as V in dialysis dosing.

    Source: Watson PE, Watson ID, Batt RD, Am J Clin Nutr 1980;33:27–39.

  • Kt/V (Daugirdas second generation)

    Single-pool haemodialysis adequacy from pre and post urea, session time and ultrafiltration.

    Source: Daugirdas JT, J Am Soc Nephrol 1993;4:1205–13; KDOQI Hemodialysis Adequacy Update, 2015.

  • Urea reduction ratio

    Percentage fall in urea over one haemodialysis session.

    Source: Lowrie EG, Lew NL, Am J Kidney Dis 1991; KDOQI Hemodialysis Adequacy Update, 2015.

  • Renal resistive index

    Doppler index of intrarenal vascular resistance from peak systolic and end-diastolic velocity.

    Source: Pourcelot L, 1974; Tublin ME, Bude RO, Platt JF, AJR Am J Roentgenol 2003;180:885–92.
